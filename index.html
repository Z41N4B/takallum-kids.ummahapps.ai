تَكَلُّم Takallum Kids: first Arabic words and sentences

Kids

About Takallum Kids (for grown-ups)

Takallum Kids (تَكَلُّم, “speaking”) is a free Arabic speaking app for children aged about 4 to 11. It is the children’s version of Takallum. Children learn a handful of words first, each with a picture and audio, and then say short sentences built from those words.

How it works

  • Meet the words. Each unit starts with 5 to 8 words. Your child sees the picture, the vowelled Arabic and the English, and hears it.
  • Say the words. Your child sees the picture, says the Arabic out loud, taps Show me to see and hear it, then taps I said it! or Try again. Words to try again come back a little later.
  • Say the sentences. Two to four word sentences made from the words, like هَذَا قِطٌّ (this is a cat), أُحِبُّ أُمِّي (I love my mum) and أَيْنَ الْكُرَةُ؟ (where is the ball?).
  • Build the sentences. Your child taps the words of each sentence in the right order, hearing each word as they tap it.
  • Warm-up. Once a day, up to eight words and sentences come back. Your child hears a word and picks its picture, or builds a sentence, so it can’t just be tapped through. Each one returns after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.
  • Three stars. Every unit is open from the start. Each one shows a star for learning the words, one for building the sentences, and one for making everything solid, which means right on three separate days. The third star takes about two weeks and can fade if warm-ups are skipped.
  • Practise again. Extra practice with the words and sentences that need it most, any time.
  • Echo mode. For children who can’t read Arabic yet: choose I listen when you add your child, and each word and sentence is spoken first so they can say it back.

Twenty-five units

My family, Hello, Food, Animals, Colours, At home, Every day, Yes no and who, People I love, My body, School and things, Yummy food, Outside, More animals, How I feel, Getting ready, At the table, Helping at home, Bedtime, Playing together and Kind words, then four units from the ahadith: Asking and answering, Good manners, Duas and remembering Allah, and Good character. That makes 177 words and 184 sentences in all. The hadith units keep the wording of Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, show each hadith number, and do not change for a boy or a girl. To practise words with games, use Kalimat; come here to make them into sentences.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It is free, has no adverts and needs no sign-up. Progress is saved in this browser on this device.

What age is it for?

About 4 to 11. It is made for children who can read Arabic letters with vowel marks, and to be used on their own. Younger children can lean on the pictures and the audio.

Which kind of Arabic does it teach?

Modern Standard Arabic (fusha), the Arabic of books and school, understood across the Arab world. Every word is fully vowelled.

Does it have boy and girl forms?

Yes. Each child picks I’m a boy or I’m a girl when they add themselves, and you can change it by tapping their name at the top. Sentences about themselves change, like أَنَا جَائِعٌ and أَنَا جَائِعَةٌ (I am hungry), and so does the praise: أَحْسَنْتَ or أَحْسَنْتِ.

How do I know my child really knows the words?

Look for the third star on each unit. All units are open, so a child can move on whenever they like, but the third star only comes from getting every word and sentence right in the warm-up on three separate days. There is no microphone check, because phones often mishear children, so saying a word out loud is on trust and the warm-up does the checking.

Why is there no sound?

The app speaks with your device’s own Arabic text-to-speech voice, and many phones, especially Samsung, don’t have one until you add it. Tap No sound? How to add an Arabic voice on the home screen for steps for your device.

Can several children use the same device?

Yes. Each child adds themselves with a name, boy or girl, and an animal, and keeps their own progress. Tap the name at the top to switch child or add another. Progress stays in this browser on this device; nothing is sent anywhere. To start one child over, open settings and choose Start again.
